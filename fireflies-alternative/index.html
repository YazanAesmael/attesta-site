The best Fireflies alternatives in 2026

Guides · Updated June 2026

Fireflies is a capable meeting assistant, but it works by sending a bot to join your call, which is the exact thing many people want to avoid. The common reasons to switch: no uninvited bot, recording in-person conversations, privacy, and pricing that is not per-seat. Here are the best options.

Why people switch from Fireflies

  • The bot shows up uninvited. A meeting assistant joining the call can be awkward or embarrassing, and it announces to everyone that the meeting is being recorded by a third party.
  • It is online-only. No help for a conversation happening in the room.
  • Per-seat pricing and lock-in, including sign-in restrictions some users dislike.
  • Privacy. People want control over where meeting data goes and whether it trains a model.

The best alternatives

Attesta — best for in-person, no bot

Records on your own iPhone with an audible consent tone, no bot joining anything. You get a speaker-labeled transcript plus a summary, action items, and decisions. It never trains AI on your audio, you own the recording, and it is pay-per-minute instead of per-seat. Built for the conversation in the room. (Disclosure: this is our app.)

Granola — best for desktop meeting notes without a bot

Listens on your Mac during an online meeting and writes the notes, without sending a bot into the call. A favorite of founders and consultants.

Otter — best if you still want online-meeting coverage

Does live transcription and notes for Zoom and Teams. Per-seat subscription; check its data-training terms.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a Fireflies alternative without a bot?

Yes. Attesta records on your own device with no bot, and Granola listens on your desktop without joining the call. Both avoid the uninvited-participant problem.

What is the best Fireflies alternative for in-person meetings?

Fireflies is built for online calls. Attesta is phone-first: put it on the table, tap record, and get a transcript plus summary and action items with an audible consent tone.

Is Fireflies a privacy concern?

Some people dislike a bot auto-joining and how meeting data is handled. If that is your concern, choose a tool that records on your own device and does not train AI on your data. Attesta does both, and you own your recordings.
