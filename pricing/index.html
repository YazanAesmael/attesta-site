Honest, pay-per-minute pricing

Updated June 2026

Most transcription tools charge a per-seat subscription whether you record once or a hundred times. Attesta does not. You get a free trial, then you pay for the minutes you actually record, and those minutes never expire. We are paid to transcribe your audio, not to mine it.

The plans

PlanPriceWhat you get
Free trial$0Free minutes on sign-in, so you can record and see a real transcript plus summary before paying.
Top-up minutes$1.99 / 30 minPay-as-you-go. Buy minutes and use them whenever. They never expire. Available with or without Pro.
Pro Monthly$9.99 / mo300 minutes a month, plus cloud sync and backup across your devices.
Pro Annual$79.99 / yr300 minutes a month, billed yearly (about 33% less than monthly), plus sync and backup.

Prices shown in the App Store are always the source of truth and are localized to your region.

What makes it honest

  • Cancel a recording before transcription starts and it costs nothing. No minute is metered until the audio is actually sent to transcribe.
  • Minutes are metered from real audio, not rounded up or padded.
  • No per-seat lock-in. You are not paying monthly for a seat you barely use.
  • Cancel Pro in one tap from your App Store subscriptions. No hoops, no retention maze.
  • Your audio is never used to train AI, and you own your recordings and transcripts.

Which should you pick?

If you record occasionally, top-up minutes are the cheapest path, pay a couple of dollars when you need them, nothing when you do not. If you record regularly (several times a week), Pro's monthly bundle plus cross-device sync is the better value. You can start free, try it on a real conversation, and decide after.

Frequently asked questions

Is Attesta free?

There is a free trial on sign-in, so you can record and see a real transcript and summary before paying. After that you buy minutes or subscribe to Pro.

How does pay-per-minute pricing work?

You buy minutes in small top-up units and spend them as you record. Minutes are metered from the real audio length and never expire, so you only pay for what you record.

Do my minutes expire?

No. Top-up minutes never expire. Pro plans grant a monthly bundle of minutes.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Pro is an App Store subscription you can cancel in one tap. And if you cancel a recording before transcription starts, no minute is charged.
