Delete Your Account

Last updated July 14, 2026

This page explains how to delete your Attesta account and the data associated with it, and exactly what is removed and what is retained. You can delete your account yourself from inside the app, or by email.

Option 1 — In the app (immediate)

The fastest way. Your account and its data are removed right away.

  1. Open Attesta and make sure you’re signed in.
  2. Open the side menu and tap Settings.
  3. Tap Account.
  4. Tap Delete account.
  5. Confirm on the “Delete your account?” sheet. Your recordings, transcripts, and insights are permanently removed and you are signed out. This can’t be undone.

Option 2 — By email

If you can’t access the app, email legal@attesta.cc from the email address linked to your account and ask us to delete it.

  • For your security we only act on requests from the account’s own email address — we won’t delete an account based on a name or handle alone.
  • We complete email deletion requests within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your audio recordings stored on our servers
  • Your transcripts and the AI summaries and insights generated from them
  • Your profile — the email address and display name from your Apple or Google sign-in
  • Your minute balance and usage history
  • Device and app information we stored to support you (device model, OS version, app version, and similar diagnostics)
  • Your sign-in itself — the account is hard-deleted, so every session stops working immediately

Recordings made on the free tier are never stored on our servers in the first place (only the transcript is returned to your device), so there is nothing server-side to delete for those.

What is retained, and why

After deletion, no data that identifies you remains in normal use. A few narrow exceptions exist:

  • Purchase and accounting records — kept as long as tax and accounting law require (typically up to 7 years). These are billing records, not your recordings.
  • A limited, access-restricted copy is held for a short period for fraud prevention, security, and so we can reverse an accidental deletion if you contact us — then it is deleted. It is not readable in the app, and it does not include your audio.
  • Encrypted database backups are cycled out automatically; any residual copy of your data is gone within 90 days.
  • Anonymized analytics — aggregate usage statistics that are not tied to your identity may be retained. They never contain your audio, transcripts, name, or email address.

Your subscription is separate — cancel it too

Deleting your Attesta account does not cancel your subscription. Subscriptions are billed by the app store, not by Attesta, and keep renewing until you cancel them where you bought them:

  • iPhone, iPad, or Mac: Settings → your Apple ID → Subscriptions → Attesta → Cancel.
  • Android: open Google Play → your profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Attesta → Cancel.

Cancel before deleting your account if you want to be certain billing has stopped.
