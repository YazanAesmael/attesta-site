Questions worth asking before you record someone.

Is it legal to record a conversation?

Recording laws vary by place. Some require everyone's consent, some only one person's. Attesta's audible tone announces every recording, which keeps you on the clean side of two-party-consent rules and is simply the honest thing to do. You're responsible for following the law where you are.

Where do my recordings go, and do you train on them?

Recordings are transcribed in the cloud and tied to your account. They are never used to train any model and never sold. You can export or delete them anytime.

Do I need to invite a bot or set up a meeting link?

No. Attesta records the conversation in the room from your iPhone. Nothing joins your calls and no one gets a notification that a bot has appeared.

Is it really free to start?

Your first 5 minutes are free, no card required. After that it's $9.99/mo, or $1.99 per 30 minutes that never expire.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone, iPad, and Mac. One purchase covers all three.
