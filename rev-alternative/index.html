The best Rev alternatives in 2026

Guides · Updated June 2026

Rev is the accuracy benchmark, especially its human transcription, but people look for an alternative for two reasons: cost (it is priced for professional work) and workflow (it is built around uploading finished files on the web, not recording on your phone). Here are the strongest options and where each fits.

Why people switch from Rev

  • Price per minute adds up for regular use, and pay-per-minute can feel opaque if the cost is not clear upfront.
  • It is upload-first. Great for an existing file, awkward if you want to record the conversation live on your phone.
  • You want the audio and a structured summary, not just a transcript you paid to process.

The best alternatives

Attesta — best for recording on your phone

Records a conversation on iPhone with an audible consent tone, then returns a speaker-labeled transcript plus a summary, action items, and decisions. Pay-per-minute with credits that never expire, transparent metering from the real audio, and it never trains AI on your recordings. You keep the audio and the transcript. (Disclosure: this is our app.)

Otter — best for online meetings

Strong on Zoom and Teams with a capped free tier. Per-seat subscription, and check its terms on data training.

Notta — best for multilingual

Broad language support across web and mobile if you work across languages.

Stick with Rev when

You need the cleanest possible transcript of a critical recording and want a human in the loop. Rev's human transcription is still the accuracy leader, and that is worth paying for on high-stakes work.

Frequently asked questions

What is a cheaper alternative to Rev?

For automated transcription, Attesta is pay-per-minute with credits that never expire; Otter and Notta have capped free tiers. The trade-off is human-grade accuracy, where Rev's human option still leads.

What is the best Rev alternative for iPhone?

Rev is web-first. Attesta records and transcribes on iPhone directly, returns a speaker-labeled transcript plus a summary, and lets you keep the audio.

Does Rev train AI on your data?

Check Rev's current terms. If a no-training guarantee matters, Attesta never trains any model on your audio, transcripts, or insights.
